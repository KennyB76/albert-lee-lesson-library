When is small-angle OK?
$\sin\theta \approx \theta \approx \tan\theta$ for $\theta \lesssim 10°$ (error $\lesssim 1\%$). Above $\sim 20°$ the gap blows up — for $\theta = 60°$, $\tan\theta$ is already $\sim 60\%$ larger than $\sin\theta$.
